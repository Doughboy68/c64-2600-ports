MAKE A PROFILE

Turn your finished C64 port of a 2600 game into a profile for the 2600 → C64 builder. The profile lets anyone with the same ROM build your port, without your .prg (or any of the original game) being shared.

1. Your files

Drop your .prg and the original ROM here Both at once, or one after the other. The ROM can be the .a26 or the .zip it came in. Or click to choose files.

Nothing is uploaded: everything happens in this page.
